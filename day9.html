三、safe 還是 safety？

你寫的：
… improve campus safe safety.
safe 形容詞・安全的
a safe place　·　keep our campus safe
safety 名詞・安全
campus safety　·　improve safety
判斷法：後面還有名詞、或在 be / keep 後面 → safe。
它自己就是「一件事」（重視安全、改善安全）→ safety。
同一組：dangerous（危險的）／danger（危險）。
小測驗
Schools should pay more attention to campus ____ .
safety
pay attention to + 名詞。
Everyone wants to study in a ____ environment.
safe
後面有名詞 environment → 形容詞。
Running in the hallway is ____ .
dangerous
be 動詞後面，形容「跑步這件事」→ 形容詞。
Students should tell a teacher when they see any ____ .
danger
see 的受詞 → 名詞。
再記一個固定說法
in schools → at school 在學校
「在學校（裡發生的事）」固定說 at school，不加 s、不加 the。
everyone at school　·　accidents at school

As a student, I think campus safety is very important. If we study in a safe place, we don't have to worry about getting hurt.␣As a result, we can concentrate on our studies and other activities. However, there is a good chance that accidents might happen at school. For example, old tiles might fall off the walls, so students are likely to get hurt. Additionally, schools should deal with these problems immediately to ensure students have a safe place to concentrate on their studies.
