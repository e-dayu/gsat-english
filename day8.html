一、Day7 重寫：你進步了

  • 動詞配主詞：全對。there is a good chance、a tile falls 都寫對了。
  • 人稱不換人：全對。整段都用 we。
  • campus safety、don't have to、However 轉折，都改到位。
  • 磁磚的例子成功寫成英文，比原稿更有畫面。

剩下兩件事。今天處理完，第二段就能寫得更乾淨。

還要修 ①
名詞還在裸奔
… accident accidents might happen in schools at school.
I think school schools should pay more attention to campus safety.
… concentrate on our study studies.「學業」固定用複數 studies。
新方法：點名詞。打勾前，用手指把每個名詞點一遍，各問一句：
「有 s 嗎？還是前面有 a / the / our？」
兩個都沒有，就是裸奔。
還要修 ②
so 和 so that 不一樣
你寫的：
Old tiles might fall off the walls so that , so students are likely to get hurt.
so＝所以（結果），前面加逗號。
so that＝為了、以便（目的），後面常有 can / will。
結果　The floor is wet, so students might fall.
目的　Schools should repair old walls so that students can walk safely.
小測驗：so 還是 so that？
I was bitten by a dog, ____ I am afraid of dogs.
so
被咬 → 所以怕狗，是結果。
We should keep the stairs dry ____ no one will slip.
so that
保持乾燥，是為了沒人滑倒，是目的。
A screw was left on the ground, ____ a student stepped on it.
so
結果。
另外三個小地方
  • However, 後面一定有逗號。
  • 句號後面要空一格：hurt. Besides，不是 hurt.Besides。
  • 結尾可以加回 After all, no one wants to get injured.
As a student, I think campus safety is very important. If we study in a safe place, we don't have to worry about getting hurt. As a result, we can concentrate on our studies and other activities. However, there is a good chance that accidents might happen at school. For example, old tiles might fall off the walls, so students are likely to get hurt. For this reason, I think schools should pay more attention to campus safety. After all, no one wants to get injured.
Besides 是「此外」；這裡是「因此能專心」，用 As a result 更合邏輯。

二、第二段要寫什麼？先用中文想

109 指考原題・第二段
說明身為校園的一份子，你覺得校內成員應該採取哪些作為以維護校園安全。

和 Day5 一樣填三格，每格一個中文重點（約 10–25 字）。可以接著你的磁磚、地板例子想。

① 承接｜誰有責任？
用一句話從第一段的「問題」轉到「大家該做事」。
維護校園安全，是校園裡每個人的責任。
② 作為｜誰、做什麼？
寫兩個具體動作，最好是不同的人（學校／學生／老師）。
學校應定期檢查老舊建築並馬上修理；學生看到危險的東西要立刻告訴老師。
要寫「誰＋做什麼」，不要只寫「大家要注意安全」。
③ 效果｜這樣做能帶來什麼？
說出結果，讓建議有說服力。
這樣就能在意外發生前預防，校園會變得更安全。

三、第二段的四個句構

紅色粗字是骨架，黑色底線可以換成自己的內容。每個唸兩遍。

① 承接
To keep our campus safe, everyone should do their part.
為了＿＿，每個人都應該盡自己的本分。
To + 原形動詞, 放句首＝「為了…」。do their part＝盡本分。
② 作為
First, schools should check old buildings regularly.
Second, students should tell a teacher right away when they see something dangerous.
首先，＿＿應該＿＿。其次，＿＿應該＿＿。
should 後面一律原形動詞。主詞記得點名詞：schools、students、a teacher。
③ 效果
By doing so, we can prevent accidents before they happen.
這樣做，我們就能＿＿。
By doing so 你以前用對過，很好，繼續用。
規則：介系詞（by、before、after）後面的動詞要加 -ing。
順手練：介系詞後加 -ing
這兩句是你以前寫的，各有一個錯。
We need to think twice before trust others.
… before trusting others.
Reward yourself after finish your goals.
… after finishing your goals.
④ 收尾
If everyone does their part, our campus will be a safer place for all of us.
如果每個人都盡本分，＿＿就會＿＿。
everyone 是單數 → does（動詞配主詞）。
可能用得到的字
repair 修理　·　report 回報　·　regularly 定期
right away 馬上　·　dangerous 危險的
keep the floor dry 保持地板乾燥
run in the hallway 在走廊奔跑

To keep our campus safe, everyone should do their part. First, schools should check old buildings regularly and repair broken walls right away. Second, students should tell a teacher when they see something dangerous, such as a wet floor or a loose tile. By doing so, we can prevent accidents before they happen.
