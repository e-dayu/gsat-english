Day6 批改｜校園安全・第一段

依據：學生回填原稿（2026-11-15）。原稿保留不改，以下為老師建議。

紅色細字＝批改　灰色刪除線＝建議拿掉　紅色粗字＝要背的句構，底線可替換

先看做得好的地方

  • 開頭 As a student, I think … 直接表態，好。
  • 有因果鏈：安全 → 不用擔心 → 能專心。As a result、For example、Besides 都用對了位置。
  • 舉例具體（濕滑地板、掉在地上的螺絲），閱卷老師看得到畫面。
  • be likely to + V、concentrate on + V-ing 用法正確。

逐句批改

1As a student, I think campus'safety campus safety is very important.
名詞可以直接當形容詞修飾名詞：campus safety（校園安全）、school rules，不需要所有格。若要用所有格，寫 our school's safety，且撇號後要空一格再接下一字。
2If students we have a safe place to study, we don't need have to worry about getting hurt anytime all the time.
人稱要一致：前面 students、後面 we，同一句換人稱會讓讀者混亂。整段用 we 最自然。
don't have to（不必）比 don't need to 更常用在作文。anytime 偏口語，all the time（一直、隨時）語意更準。
3As a result,  we can concentrate on studying, improve our sport ability, among others our studies and other activities, such as sports.
① As a result, 後只空一格。
② among others 是「（在…之中）還有其他人／物」，要接在名詞後，例如 Taipei, among others，不能當「等等」放句尾。
③ concentrate on 後面接的東西要同一種形式（平行結構）：our studies and sports。原句 concentrate on studying, improve… 把兩個動作硬接，缺 and。
④ 「運動能力」：athletic ability 或 sports skills，沒有 sport ability。
4However, in school, there are is a great likelihood good chance that we might get hurt by accident.
① 主詞是 a great likelihood（單數）→ there is。看 there 後面的名詞決定 is / are。
② 前一句說「安全的好處」，這句轉到「學校其實有危險」，中間要有轉折 However,，否則邏輯像斷掉。
③ likelihood 沒錯但偏正式生硬；a good chance that … 更自然。「可能」加 might。
5For example, we will fall might slip and fall if the floors is floor is slippery.
① 主詞動詞一致：the floor is 或 the floors are，不能 floors is。
② will 表示「一定會」，太絕對；might 表示可能。slip and fall（滑倒）是固定搭配。
6Besides, if there is a screw dropping on the ground a loose screw is left on the ground, student students are likely to step on it.
① student 是可數名詞，不能單獨裸用 → students（泛指學生用複數）。
② dropping 是「正在掉落」，但你要說的是「掉在地上、留在那裡」→ is left on the ground。loose（鬆脫的）讓畫面更清楚。
7By those reasons For these reasons, I think school schools should care about the safety in school pay more attention to campus safety.
① 「基於這些理由」固定用 For these reasons；剛說完的理由用 these，不用 those。
② 泛指學校用 schools。③ 一句出現兩次 school，換成 campus safety 呼應開頭，句子更俐落。
8After all, no one want wants to get injured.
① no one 是單數 → wants（同 everyone wants、someone wants）。
② 加 After all,（畢竟）當收尾，語氣自然，不突兀。

本次文法重點（3 條就好）

  • 主詞動詞一致：there is a chance／the floor is／no one wants／students are。寫完逐句檢查一次。
  • 人稱一致：整段選定 we，就不要中途換成 students。
  • 可數名詞不裸用：student → students／a student；school → schools／our school。

要背的句構

簡單、不賣弄，但能讓段落更順。底線處換成自己的內容。

開頭表態
As a student, I believe (that) campus safety is of great importance.
身為學生，我認為＿＿非常重要。（is very important 也對；of great importance 稍正式）
說好處
If we study in a safe environment, we don't have to worry about getting hurt.
worry about 後接 V-ing。
As a result, we can focus on our studies and other activities.
轉折＋舉例
However, there is a good chance that accidents might happen at school.
然而，＿＿很有可能＿＿。（there is ＋單數名詞）
收尾
For these reasons, I think schools should pay more attention to campus safety.
After all, no one wants to get injured.
基於這些理由，＿＿應更重視＿＿。畢竟沒有人想＿＿。

建議修正版（保留你的想法）

As a student, I think campus safety is very important. If we study in a safe environment, we don't have to worry about getting hurt all the time. As a result, we can concentrate on our studies and other activities, such as sports. However, in school, there is a good chance that we might get hurt by accident. For example, we might slip and fall if the floor is slippery. Besides, if a loose screw is left on the ground, students are likely to step on it. For these reasons, I think schools should pay more attention to campus safety. After all, no one wants to get injured.
原稿意思與舉例全部保留；只改文法、搭配與轉折。
